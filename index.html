Happy 1st Anniversary, my Lover.

I don't think words will ever be enough to explain how thankful I am that I get to have you in my life, but I still want to try.

Thank you for staying. Thank you for understanding me, for making me laugh, and for being someone I can be completely comfortable with. Even though we're in a long-distance relationship, you've somehow found ways to make me feel close to you. Sometimes it's through our conversations, sometimes through the little things you do, and sometimes it's simply knowing that you're there.

I love how our relationship isn't only about the big moments. It's also about the random conversations, the silly things we talk about, the small updates throughout the day, and those ordinary moments that somehow become special because they're with you.

Our first year wasn't perfect, and I don't expect it to be. We've had misunderstandings, difficult days, busy days, and moments when the distance probably made things harder than they needed to be. But through all of that, I appreciate that we still choose to understand each other and keep going.

I appreciate the effort you put into us, even in ways you might not realize. I notice the little things. I notice when you try, when you make time, when you listen, and when you simply stay.

One of the things I love most is knowing that there's still so much about you that I haven't discovered yet. I want to know the future versions of you. The things you'll learn, the things you'll achieve, the things you'll become, and even the little habits you'll probably develop along the way.

And someday, I hope we get to experience all the ordinary things we've only imagined. Sitting beside each other. Going somewhere together. Getting food together. Laughing about something stupid in person. Having conversations without a screen between us.

Tangi, if there's one thing this first year has taught me, it's that distance doesn't stop me from caring about you. It doesn't stop me from missing you, appreciating you, or imagining a future where we're finally in the same place.

Happy first anniversary, Austin.

Thank you for being part of my little universe, for making this first year something I'll always remember, and for giving me so many reasons to be grateful.

Happy 1st Anniversary, my Lover.

Happy December 5, my Tangi.
I love you, and I'm so grateful that I get to call you mine.

For my Lover, Baby, and Tangi.

A Letter for Everything We Haven’t Said Yet

My Tangi,

If you made it all the way here, then I want this last letter to be different from everything else on this website. This isn't another list of reasons or another reminder of how much I love you. I want this one to be about what our first year actually felt like—the little moments that happened between two people who were trying to build something together while being miles apart. Being in an LDR means we don't get all the ordinary things other couples get to experience. We can't simply meet after a long day, sit beside each other, get food together whenever we want, or suddenly decide to go somewhere. Most of our little moments happen through a screen, through messages, calls, pictures, updates, and those simple “what are you doing?” conversations. But somehow, those little things became our version of being together.

Sometimes I think about how much of our relationship is made up of waiting. Waiting for a message, waiting for a reply, waiting for a call, waiting for the right time to talk, waiting for the day when the distance won't be there anymore. There are days when all I want is to be able to talk to you without having to think about the time or whether you're busy. I want to be able to tell you something and see your reaction right away instead of imagining it through a screen. I want to be able to sit beside you while you're playing your games instead of imagining what it would be like. I want to be able to notice the little things in person instead of learning them through messages and conversations. But even with all that distance, you still somehow became part of my everyday life.

I love how our conversations can turn into memories even when nothing special is happening. Sometimes you tell me something about your day, sometimes you send a random thought, sometimes we start talking about one thing and somehow end up somewhere completely different. Those conversations might seem small, but they're the kind of things I find myself remembering. It's funny how one message from you can become the best part of an ordinary day. Even when we're both busy or tired, just knowing that I can still hear from you makes the distance feel a little smaller.

I also love the little reminders of you that show up when you're not even around. Yellow can suddenly make me think of you. Sunflowers can remind me of you. Strawberries can make me smile because somehow they became another little connection to you. Cats can remind me of all the random conversations and little things we've shared. Even games can make me think about you because I know how much you enjoy them, and sometimes I imagine what it would be like if distance wasn't between us—if I could actually sit beside you while you play, watch you get focused, tease you a little, and laugh with you instead of just imagining it.

The gym reminds me of you too, because it makes me think about the things you put effort into and the things that matter to you. Those details might sound random when written down like this, but that's exactly why I love them. They're random things that became connected to one person. That's what happens when someone becomes important to you. You start noticing little things differently because somewhere in your mind, they remind you of that person.

One of the strangest and sweetest parts of being LDR is that I know so much about your days without physically being there. I can know when something happened because you tell me. I can notice when your messages feel different. I can hear about the things that made you laugh, the things that annoyed you, the things you were excited about, and the ordinary things you did that day. I can't always be there to experience those moments with you, but I can still listen. And sometimes I think that's one of the ways we learned to be close despite the distance—by letting each other into the small parts of our everyday lives.

There are probably so many moments you don't even realize I remember. A random conversation. A funny message. Something you told me about your day. A small update that you probably sent without thinking twice. A time when we kept talking even though there wasn't really anything important to say. A moment when one of us was tired but still wanted to talk. Those moments are easy to overlook because they aren't dramatic. But when you're in an LDR, those small moments can mean so much because they're the closest thing we have to simply being beside each other.

Sometimes I wish I could save every conversation we've ever had somewhere—not just the words, but the feeling of those moments. I wish I could remember exactly what it felt like every time I saw your name appear on my screen. I wish I could save all the random conversations, the jokes, the updates, the late talks, the little messages, and even the quiet moments when we didn't have much to say. Because someday, I know we'll look back and realize that those were our everyday moments. They were our version of sitting beside each other.

And that's what makes me look forward to the future. Not because I expect everything to suddenly become perfect, but because there are so many simple things we haven't experienced together yet. I want to know what it feels like to finally see you after spending so much time missing you through a screen. I want to know what it feels like to actually sit beside you and not have to say goodbye because the call is ending. I want to know what it feels like to eat together, walk somewhere together, laugh beside each other, and just exist in the same place.

Maybe our first time being together in person won't be some perfect movie scene. Maybe we'll be nervous. Maybe we'll laugh because it feels strange finally seeing each other after being used to a screen. Maybe we won't know what to say at first. And honestly, I think that's okay. Because after everything we've gone through while being apart, simply being there would already mean so much.

I imagine the little things more than the big ones. I imagine sitting somewhere with you and talking about things we've already talked about a hundred times. I imagine getting food and arguing over what to choose. I imagine walking around without needing a reason to go anywhere. I imagine watching you play your games while I'm actually beside you. I imagine seeing the little expressions that I've only been able to imagine through messages and calls. I imagine laughing at something completely stupid and thinking, “Finally, I'm actually here.”

Those are the memories I look forward to—not necessarily expensive dates or huge surprises, but ordinary moments that we haven't been able to have yet. Because when you've spent so much time apart, ordinary things start to feel extraordinary. Sitting beside someone becomes special. Eating together becomes special. Walking together becomes special. Even doing absolutely nothing together becomes something worth remembering.

Our first year taught me that love doesn't always look like being physically close. Sometimes it looks like waiting for a reply. Sometimes it looks like making time even when you're tired. Sometimes it looks like sending a random update because you want the other person to know what you're doing. Sometimes it looks like staying on a call a little longer. Sometimes it looks like remembering small details. Sometimes it looks like missing someone and still choosing to stay connected. Sometimes it simply looks like two people saying, in their own little ways, “I'm still here.”

And I think that's one of the things I'll always remember about this year. We didn't get to experience everything together physically, but we still found ways to make each other part of our days. We created memories out of conversations. We turned notifications into little moments of happiness. We turned ordinary updates into pieces of our story. We made a year out of all those small moments that happened while we were miles apart.

December 5 will always remind me of that. It's just a date, but now it carries an entire year behind it. A year of screens and messages, calls and waiting, laughter and random conversations, missing each other, learning each other, and continuing to choose to be part of each other's lives. One day, I hope December 5 becomes more than a date we celebrate through a screen. I hope someday we can look at that date while sitting beside each other and say, “We really made it through that first year.”

There are still so many things I want to experience with you. So many conversations we haven't had, places we haven't gone, foods we haven't shared, pictures we haven't taken, jokes we haven't made, and memories we haven't created yet. This website might have 365 reasons, but I know there are still thousands more reasons and memories waiting for us.

So if you ever miss me, I hope you remember that somewhere in all these little pages is proof of how many times you crossed my mind. Every color, every flower, every random reminder, every little note, every memory, and every word here came from somewhere. They came from the small pieces of our first year that made me think, “This reminds me of you.”

And when the distance feels especially far, I hope you remember that the distance is only one part of our story. It isn't every conversation. It isn't every laugh. It isn't every little moment we shared. It isn't everything we've built. It's simply the space between us right now. And someday, I hope that space becomes one of the things we look back on and say, “We really had to go through all of that just to finally be here.”

Until then, I'll keep appreciating the little things. I'll keep remembering the random conversations. I'll keep smiling at the things that remind me of you. I'll keep looking forward to the day when I don't have to imagine what it would be like to sit beside you because you'll actually be there. And I'll keep believing that some of the best memories are still the ones we haven't made yet.

Happy first anniversary, my Lover. Happy 365 days, my Baby. Happy December 5, my Tangi. Thank you for being part of my ordinary days even when we couldn't physically share the same place. Thank you for every message, every call, every update, every laugh, every random conversation, and every little moment that made the distance feel smaller.

One day, I hope we can read this together while sitting beside each other. And when that day comes, I hope we'll laugh at how much we used to miss each other through a screen. I hope we'll look back at this website and remember that there was a time when being together meant opening a phone, waiting for a message, and wishing the miles between us were a little smaller.

But for now, this is our story. Two people, two places, one year, countless conversations, and so many little memories in between.

And if I could choose one thing to keep from this entire first year, it wouldn't be one particular day. It would be the feeling of knowing that even from far away, there was someone on the other side of the screen who became such a meaningful part of my everyday life.

Happy first anniversary, Tangi.

Here's to the 365 days we've already made, the distance we've learned to live with, the memories we've created from miles away, and all the days ahead when hopefully, we won't have to count the distance anymore.

I love you.

And maybe someday, when we're finally in the same place, we'll make the simplest memories of all—and those will become our favorites.

“Above all, keep loving each other earnestly.”
